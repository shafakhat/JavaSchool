## Suggested study paths

1. **Fresh graduate path:** [Core tutorial](intro.html) → topic banks in sidebar order → Test 1 → master list skim → Tests 2-3.
2. **Working dev, 2 weeks:** master list (skip what you know) → deep-dive Collections/Threads banks → Tests 2-3 timed → [Advanced Java](networking.html) reading.
3. **Legacy stack (Struts/Spring/EJB):** [Web bank](interview-web.html) → [Struts](struts.html) · [Spring](spring.html) · [EJB](ejb.html) pages → archive examples in the sidebar.
