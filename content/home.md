## Where to start?

If you are new to programming, follow this path:

1. [Java Introduction](intro.html) - understand the language in 5 minutes
2. [Environment Setup](install.html) - get `javac` and `java` working
3. [Java Syntax](syntax.html) - the shape of every Java program
4. [Variables](variables.html) and [Operators](operators.html) - the building blocks
5. [Control Flow](conditionals.html) - decisions and loops
6. [Classes and Objects](classes-objects.html) - start thinking in objects

Already know the basics? Jump to [Collections](collections.html), [Streams](streams.html) or take the [Quiz](quiz.html).
