In programming, a statement is an instruction to do something. It controls the sequence of execution of a program. In Java, a statement is terminated with a semicolon and multiple statements can be written on a single line.

```java title=Example.java
x = y + 1; z = y + 2;
```

In Java, an empty statement is legal and does nothing:

```java title=Example.java
;
```
